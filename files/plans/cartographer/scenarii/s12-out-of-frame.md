> contrainte. Des contraintes, on en aura … n'en invente pas. »* The dev's remark of 2026-09-22 was about what the
> map shows (too far east, the Sahel not our business); the **editorial zone** — a second box per year, an import
> filter, a removal button, "outside the subject" marks — was Claude's elaboration of it, and nothing used it: the

> anything.** What the render frame shows is the DEM's affair, unchanged.


> [Index](s00-index.md) · the DEM frame reaches **E 78.75** (the Indus) and **S 11.18** (the Sahel),
> because the relief is deliberately wider than the subject — the Total War out-of-frame look. The
> political subject is narrower, and the two must stop being the same thing.

## The decision — 2026-09-22

The dev: the frame shows too much to the east; it should stop around **Iran and the Caspian**; the
sub-Saharan Sahel is **not our business by default**, though the possibility must stay open — *"à part à
partir du XVIIIe siècle, ou progressivement."*

**Two separate things, and only the second is cheap:**

1. **The render frame** — the DEM and the land mask, 3 MB each, generated by `files/spikes/fetch-dem`.
   Changing it means regenerating both assets and reframing the camera. A chantier of its own, and the
   out-of-frame relief is wanted.
2. **The editorial zone** — where we draw polities at all. **A filter at import**, costing nothing.

## Measured, 2026-09-22

Proposed zone: **lon −45…60, lat 20…76** — Europe, the whole Mediterranean including Egypt, Anatolia,
the Levant, stopping before Iran and the Sahara.

At 1100: **45 polities inside, 6 outside.**

| outside | at |
|---|---|
| Ghana Empire | 8.7°W, 15.7°N |
| Kanem–Bornu Empire | 16.0°E, 15.6°N |
| Sind Sultanate | 68.8°E, 26.1°N |
| Gondwana Kingdom | 81.5°E, 21.2°N |
| Kahlur State | 76.7°E, 31.4°N |
| **Eastern Settlement** | **46.6°W**, 60.6°N |

**The Fatimids, Abbasids and Zirids fall inside**, which is right: they hold Egypt and the Levant and they
are the other half of the Crusader states already in the payload.

**One to settle**: `Eastern Settlement` is the Norse Greenland colony. It falls out to the *west*, not
because of this zone but because the DEM frame starts at −45. Keep it or not is a subject question, not a
zone one.

> **Clipping geometry at the zone would be wrong**: it invents a border that never existed. The zone
> decides *what we author*, never *what shape a polity has*.
