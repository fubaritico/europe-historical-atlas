**Kievan Rus' (lot vassals-01)**: Rostov, Suzdal and Smolensk **agree with the map** — Monomakh's patrimony, confirmed
at Liubech in 1097 (the *Primary Chronicle*); Smolensk held by his son Sviatoslav c. 1097–1113 (Brockhaus and Efron).

**Divergences kept**: the east-bank part of the county of Chalon — held of the king of Burgundy (Longnon) or left to
France (Fournier), which bears on Louhans and Seurre. The lords of Bâgé sovereign (Guichenon, known only through
Courtépée, who rejects it). Mâcon's count in 1100: Étienne I or Guillaume II (the Wikipedia list gives both).
