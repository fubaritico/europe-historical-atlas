**The remedy**: Bautzen, Görlitz and the land between them (and the Nisan district round Dresden) out of the
Margraviate of Meissen, into the Duchy of Bohemia — or a vassal of Bohemia for Wiprecht's land, if the dev wants the
lord shown. Görlitz rests on its district: hatching is fair if the dev wants to show it.
