# The Slavic lands between Elbe and Oder in 1100 — what the sources say against the map

Lot A, 2026-11-21, researched by one background agent of the historian's session, then reread and entered by the
historian. The work: `files/resources/historien/1100/regions/wends-1100-research/` — `quotes.json` (97 quotations, each
copied by script from a downloaded text), `places.json` (30 places), `reports/lot-a.md` (one block per place),
`under.json` (what the map draws under each).

**Entered on 2026-11-21** (the dev: the witness points first, the drawing after): 26 places and their 1100 sheets
(places 13660–13685, sheets 13678–13703), sources 5463–5500, territories 373–378 (Abodrites and Lutici as
confederations; Rani, Hevelli, Brizani, Ukrani as tribes — the kind is indicative, the dev sets the ranks when he
draws), people 506–511, titles 398–403, the link Henry → Magnus Billung (283). The Latin lines entered (Helmold I, 2, 34,
36, 37, 38; the annals of Magdeburg and Rosenfeld at 1100) were **read by eye on the page images** and are entered as
printed, not as the OCR gave them; the Wikipedia lines were checked by script against the downloaded pages (61 of 61).

**Four of the 30 not entered**, no attestation near 1100: Potsdam (993 only), Rugard (« wahrscheinlich »), Ilow (first
dated 1164–1166), Köpenick (mid-12th century). **Sub-tribes not created** (Wagrians, Polabians, Kessini, Circipani,
Tollensi, Redarii, Linones, Warnower, Dossani, Sprewani): no sheet names them as holder; they are in the table below.

**Holders entered where the readings diverge**: Güstrow and Demmin under the Lutici (Helmold counts the Kessini and
Circipani among them), with Henry's rule over both tribes kept as a second line; Brandenburg under the Hevelli, with
Udo's capture of 1100 kept as two lines (a date is not a year: taken during 1100, how long held is not said); Pasewalk
under the Ukrani, the Pomeranian reading kept.

## For the dev's map

The map of 1100 draws **no polity** over the whole zone (29 of the 30 places lie on unclaimed land; Köpenick lies in
the Saxon Eastern March). The sources read put four groups there.

## Hypotheses, and how far each was read

| 1 | Henry held Wagria, Polabia and the Obotrites from 1093, under the duke of Saxony | Helmold I, 34 in Latin (OCR) | supported by one primary witness; Wikipedia agrees (same witness: Helmold) |
| 2 | All the eastern Slavs paid Henry tribute « from that day » (1093) | Helmold I, 34 (`H34c`) | one witness |
| 3 | The Kessini and Circipani came under Henry only in 1121, after Duke Lothar's campaign | Wikipedia (de) « Kessiner » (`W-kessiner-2`), which cites the Annalista Saxo a. 1121 | Annalista Saxo **not read** (MGH SS VI not found in open text) |

| 7 | The duke of Saxony held the land directly (a « Billung march ») | Wikipedia (de) « Mark der Billunger »: a modern coinage, its existence disputed | not supported for 1100 |

Malchow, Kessin (its page not found), Bützow, Parchim, Tribsees, Groswin, Stolpe, Gadebusch, Wittenburg, Pritzerbe,

## Left open after the entry

- The Rani's king and the Hevellian prince of 1100 have no name in the texts read: each is entered as a collective
  (« Rois des Ranes », « Princes des Hévelles »), like the Lutici, Brizani and Ukrani chiefs.
- The Lutici, Rani, Hevelli, Brizani and Ukrani titles are ticked sovereign; for the Ukrani and the Brizani no line
  says it in so many words — fragile.
- Lenzen is entered under Henry on the Obotrite prince Gottschalk's tie to the place (1066) alone; Helmold I, 37 has
  Henry's son raiding the Lini: a second pass should read it.
- The four links added by hand at the end of `vassalage.md` are to be carried into the lot reports before that table
  is regenerated.
