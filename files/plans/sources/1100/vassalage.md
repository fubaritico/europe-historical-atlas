# 1100 — the register of vassalage

> The dev, 2026-11-14: *"we must have the tree of vassalage per place"*. Every place carries its holder and the chain
> above it up to the sovereign; a point in the Cartographer is valid only when the chain climbs without a gap to the
> duchy drawn under it and to that duchy's suzerain. **Each link is established once for 1100, here**, and reused by
> every place under that lord. Read before a research lot, extended after it (`/find-sources`, `/enter-in-cms`). Moves
> into the base when it can carry a link (CMS-422); CARTO-435 climbs it.

A link is **proven** when a witness read says this vassal held of that suzerain in 1100 (or across it); **unproven**
when it rests on a deduction (no prince between, the office, the region) — to be proven by a later lot. A doubt keeps
both readings.
