## Every place, the same three answers

Did it exist in 1100 · whom did it answer to (the nearest lord, never a mere owner) · the chain of that lord up to the
sovereign, each link proven once in the year's register `vassalage.md` — the dev's rule of 2026-11-14.

## The notes, in smaller files

From this night, a region's note is a folder, one `.md` per lot or district —
`files/plans/sources/1100/<region>/<district>.md` — with an index `README.md` in the folder listing the districts, the
places entered, the divergences with the map and what is to read in the browser. The large notes of before
(`germany-east.md`, `hungary.md`, `adriatic.md`, `italy.md`) stay as they are until the dev says how he wants to read
them (his idea: the `.md` shown as pages in a browser).
